# unimux 1.1.2

- **Switch Claude accounts automatically at a limit.** When the main account reaches the threshold of its 5-hour (default 95 %) or 7-day limit (default 98 %), unimux switches to the saved account with the most room, or to one you choose, right away or after you confirm in a notification. Accounts over a threshold are never picked, the primary account comes back after its reset, and when every account is over the threshold you are told once when the next reset is. Turn it on with the **Switcher** switch in the header of the Accounts & limits panel and set the mode, target and primary account, the thresholds and returning home in its card (a click on the state line under the header, or a right click on the switch); the thresholds and the pause between switches are in Settings > Accounts & limits.
- **Several Codex accounts.** "Dodaj konto Codex" keeps every account in its own folder with its own limits, and "Ustaw jako główne" puts its sign-in into Codex without touching your sessions, history or configuration. Codex accounts can take part in the automatic switch too.
- **Accounts & limits.** The AI account limits panel is now called Accounts & limits and has its own settings section with the panel, the switcher and the limit warnings (they used to sit under Notifications and sounds). Every threshold is a slider, so any value from 1 to 100 % can be chosen (for example 50 %), not only fixed steps.
- **A resizable limits panel.** Drag the top edge of the Accounts & limits panel to make it taller or shorter; a double click restores it.
- **The first Claude sign-in on a new computer.** A computer with only Claude Desktop no longer reports "To konto jest już dodane" for an account that is nowhere: the first sign-in adds the account and offers to make it the main one. The sign-in window tells you to paste the code from the browser and has a "Wklej kod" button, and without Claude Code (or Codex) installed it offers to install it in the app and signs in right after.
- **A new AI team panel.** Tasks grouped as in progress, open and done with who gave what to whom, a detail view with the result and quick actions, threads of messages per agent with a reply box, an agent filter, and an inline form for a new task, in the app's current look.
- **Faster MCP for agents.** `wait_for` wakes up on the event without polling, reports an agent that exited, and keeps long waits alive with progress notifications; the window gets only what changed, batched, and `read_inbox` no longer repeats task instructions an agent has already seen.
- **No red badge on the Windows taskbar.** The taskbar button stays as it is; the window still flashes when an agent needs you, and the macOS Dock keeps its count.

Validated with the full test suite and checks in the built app (fake Claude and Codex accounts with a fake limits server for the automatic switch, a first sign-in with a Claude Desktop leftover and without Claude Code installed, live MCP clients for the AI team panel and `wait_for`).
