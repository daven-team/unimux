# unimux 1.2.6

Sessions that keep going after an account switch, an expired subscription shown as one, a heads-up before a login ends, fresh limits right after start, and a smarter account switcher.

- **Sessions continue after an account switch.** Claude Code 2.1.328 stops its automatic continue at the usage limit when the login changes ("Automatic continue stopped · your login changed or ended", or "Workflow paused" for a workflow) and waits for a prompt. unimux now sends `continue` to that session itself when the new account is signed in and has room and the session sits idle at an empty prompt, whoever switched the account (unimux, claude-swap or you). At most once per stop. Turn it off in Settings > Accounts.
- **An expired subscription says so.** A saved account whose subscription ended no longer shows "Access denied (HTTP 403)" under empty bars for days. After a 403 the app asks the account's profile once; a lapsed subscription shows "Subscription expired" with "Remove account" right on the card, is never a switch target and is checked again every 6 hours in case it is renewed. The plan badge follows the profile (Pro, Max 5x, Max 20x, Ultra, Team, Enterprise, Free).
- **A heads-up before a login ends.** In the last week of a stored Claude login the card shows "Sign in again within 5 d 12 h", and "Sign in again" is in its menu.
- **Fresh limits right after start.** The first read after opening unimux reads every account at once, also those that were waiting after an error, so the percentages and resets are current from the start. Only an account the usage service rate-limited still waits.
- **Choose how the switcher picks the account.** Settings > Accounts > "Account choice": the one with the most room (as before) or the one whose weekly limit resets soonest, so the quota that would expire anyway is used first.
- **A way out when every account is over its line.** When the main account hits 100% and every other account is past its threshold, the switcher moves to the one with the most room left (at least 3 points), instead of staying on the exhausted one. In "Ask" mode it offers it.
- **Accounts without a 5-hour window** (Ultra) are judged on their weekly window alone.
- **Safer next to claude-swap 1.1.** Taking over an abandoned sign-in lock now goes through claude-swap's takeover step, so the two apps can never both take one dead lock; claude-swap 1.1 rosters are read correctly; and a legacy `~/.claude/.config.json` is used exactly where Claude Code uses it.

Validated with the Rust and TypeScript test suites on Windows and macOS and the checks of the generated message tables.
