# unimux 1.2.7

An unpaid subscription told as one with a way to pay, access-denied cards that look like every other sign-in problem, clearer diagnostics, and dependency updates.

- **An unpaid subscription says so.** When Claude refuses an account's limits because its subscription is unpaid (overdue invoice), the card shows "Subscription unpaid" with a "Pay" button that opens the overdue invoice on claude.ai. The limits come back on their own within half an hour after paying. If it is the main account, the switcher moves to another account at once instead of waiting.
- **Access denied looks like an expired sign-in.** A card refused with HTTP 403 no longer shows old bars at 0% under the error and a "Sign in again within ..." line next to it: it shows one line, "Access denied (HTTP 403)", and "Sign in again", dimmed like an expired sign-in, with "Remove account" in its menu.
- **Diagnostics that say something.** "Copy diagnostics" now gives the HTTP answer of the latest read (200, the error code, or "no answer (network)") instead of n/a, the next regular read ("in 3 min (regular read every 5 min)") as well as the end of a hold, and the subscription state the account's profile reported.
- **Dependency updates.** hono and @hono/node-server (development tooling only) are updated past six advisories; dompurify, marked, vite, vitest, the Tauri CLI and API, zustand and PGlite are updated, and @types/node follows Node 26.

Validated with the Rust and TypeScript test suites on Windows and macOS and the checks of the generated message tables.
