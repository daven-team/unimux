# unimux 1.2.5

A Projects panel that works like Cursor's Explorer, folders dragged in from Explorer, a clearer question before a terminal link opens, and no more doubled tooltips.

- **Add from the panel header.** Hover the Projects panel: its header shows "Add project", "New folder" and "Collapse / expand all folders", like the Explorer header in Cursor. The two dashed buttons under the list are gone.
- **Drag folders in from Explorer.** Drop a folder from Explorer (or Finder) on the Projects panel to add it as a project; drop it on a project folder to add it straight into that folder. Files are skipped with a note.
- **A Cursor-like project menu.** No title row and no icons, short labels and shortcuts at the right: open in Cursor or Explorer, new grid, files, history, "Move to folder ›" (any folder, a new one, or out of its folder), the colours, copy path, edit name and icon, change folder, remove from the list. Moving up and down is dragging.
- **Folders tidy themselves up.** A folder goes away with its last project (moved out, dropped elsewhere or removed from the list). "New folder" asks for the name first, so cancelling leaves nothing behind.
- **No accidental renames.** Clicking a folder only folds or unfolds it; a quick second click on its arrow no longer opens the rename (rename it from its menu).
- **The link question, redone.** The address sits in its own box with its host in bold, so you see at a glance where the link leads; the extra warning sentence is gone. "Copy" copies the link instead of opening it, and Enter opens it.
- **Don't ask again for this address.** Tick it once and links to that host (with its port, for example localhost:3000) open from the terminal right away. Every other address still asks. Settings > General lists the trusted addresses; remove one to be asked again.
- **One tooltip at a time.** Windows' own tooltip never shows next to the app's any more (it appeared over the account name with the organization of its card). The project tooltip is just its path.
- **No double ring on dialog buttons.** The focused button of a dialog is shown by a lighter background and a thin inner line instead of a second outline around it, in every dialog of the app.
- **Remove an expired main login's card.** The card of a Codex or Claude main login whose sign-in expired now has "Remove account" in its menu, like the saved accounts. It takes the card off the limits panel without touching the CLI's own login; the card comes back when you sign in again.

Validated with the Rust and TypeScript test suites on Windows and macOS and the checks of the generated message tables.
